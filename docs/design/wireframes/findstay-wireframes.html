FindStay — Sprint 1 Wireframes (S1-01-T4)

Grey lo-fi layouts, phone-first (360px) with desktop variant (≥1024px). Source: SCR-* inventory in docs/design/user-flows.md (S1-01-T3).

Numbered dots = element; legend under each screen says what it does. Status is never colour-only (icon + label, NFR-07). Prices GH₵ (stored pesewas); times Africa/Accra (stored UTC). Dashed boxes = images.

Out of Sprint 1 (not drawn): SCR-25A Recently Viewed, SCR-26 Booking Details, SCR-27 Confirm Booking, SCR-28 Booking Confirmation. Loading/empty/error/success states follow the Screen-State Matrix (§7) and are not redrawn per screen.
