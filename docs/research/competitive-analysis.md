# FindStay — Competitive Analysis

## 1. Overview
This document analyses existing accommodation marketplaces, property-management systems (PMS), and local tourism platforms to identify where current solutions fall short for the **Ghanaian** market and how **FindStay** bridges the gap.

---

## 2.3 Review of Existing Accommodation Platforms

### 2.3.1 Airbnb
Airbnb illustrates a guest-to-host accommodation marketplace. Its public host resources describe tools for managing listings, calendars, pricing, reservations, guest communication, and payouts. It also documents identity verification and review-related trust mechanisms. Airbnb demonstrates how a marketplace can combine property discovery, booking, payment handling, communication, and host participation. The publicly reviewed materials do not establish a complete income-and-expense accounting function for every host, nor do they establish a Ghana-specific administrative verification process. These are areas FindStay is designed to address through owner financial tools and locally appropriate review procedures.

### 2.3.2 Booking.com
Booking.com is an online travel marketplace serving travellers and accommodation partners. Its partner documentation describes partner registration and property-management activities, while its payment documentation describes different payment arrangements, including Payments by Booking and Pay at Property. The platform demonstrates broad accommodation discovery, reservation management, guest reviews, and distribution. However, the reviewed documentation does not establish a full, Ghana-focused owner income-and-expense ledger or a local platform-administration process of the kind proposed for FindStay.

### 2.3.3 Hostelworld
Hostelworld is relevant because FindStay includes hostels and budget accommodation. It demonstrates the value of a platform tailored to hostel discovery and property participation. Its public documentation and integration guidance show that booking-payment collection arrangements can vary between the platform and the accommodation property. The available reviewed sources do not establish a comprehensive income-and-expense management system for participating hostel owners. FindStay will apply accommodation-unit and booking concepts across hostels, guesthouses, hotels, and serviced apartments rather than serving only one property type.

### 2.3.4 Ghana Tourism Marketplace (GTM)
The Ghana Tourism Marketplace (GTM) is relevant national context. The Ghana Tourism Development Company states that GTM, managed in collaboration with the Ghana Tourism Authority, aggregates tourism and hospitality vendors in Ghana on a unified platform for distributing their products and services. This establishes that FindStay is not the first digital initiative in Ghana's tourism space. However, the reviewed official GTM description does not provide sufficient detail to establish its full accommodation-booking, payment, owner-management, and financial-reporting capabilities.

## 2.4 Review of Existing Property-Management Systems (PMS)

### 2.4.1 LodgeStatus
LodgeStatus presents a broad hotel-management platform. Its public feature information covers property and room configuration, booking calendars, direct booking, walk-ins and reservations, check-in/check-out, payments and refunds, receipts, staff roles, guest information, maintenance, and revenue analytics. It demonstrates that a locally relevant hospitality system can bring operational and financial activities together. It is principally presented as a system for a hotel's own operations rather than a public multi-property accommodation marketplace with a platform-level verification process.

### 2.4.2 NightsBridge
NightsBridge provides accommodation technology for providers in African markets. Its public information describes front-desk booking, a booking engine, channel management, payment solutions, PMS integration, guest communication, and availability updates across connected channels. It demonstrates the value of centralised availability and direct booking for accommodation businesses. It is focused on helping an individual accommodation provider manage and distribute its own inventory rather than operating as a Ghana-focused consumer marketplace with a centralised property-verification workflow.

### 2.4.3 Little Hotelier and Cloudbeds
Little Hotelier targets small accommodation businesses, while Cloudbeds serves accommodation operators with property-management, booking-engine, and reporting capabilities. Both show the importance of availability control, direct booking, booking records, operational reporting, and user permissions for accommodation providers. Their public product descriptions are useful design references for FindStay's owner-facing functions. They are not presented as a Ghana-specific public marketplace with the platform-level local administration proposed for FindStay.

## 2.6 Identified Gap & FindStay Positioning
The reviewed systems show that accommodation marketplaces and property-management systems serve complementary purposes. Marketplaces emphasise public discovery, reservations, reviews, and provider participation, while PMS products emphasise operational management, availability, direct booking, payments, and reporting.

**FindStay** is proposed to combine relevant capabilities from both categories in one coordinated Ghana-oriented platform:
1. **Guest Marketplace:** Search, compare, reserve with double-booking protection, pay via Mobile Money or card, and leave verified post-stay reviews.
2. **Owner Management Portal:** Manage properties, individual rooms/units, availability calendars, staff permissions, income/expenses, and operational reports.
3. **Platform Governance:** Administer Ghana Card & GTA licence verification, listing approvals, user moderation, and immutable Act 843 audit logs.
