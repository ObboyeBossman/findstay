# FindStay — Project Overview

## 1. Product Vision
**A marketplace that keeps property operations together.**

For **small and medium accommodation owners and managers**, **FindStay** is a Ghana-focused accommodation marketplace and property-management system that helps them attract verified guests, prevent double-bookings, accept Mobile Money and card payments, and manage occupancy and records in one mobile-friendly platform. Unlike agents, global booking platforms, and standalone PMS software, FindStay brings local verification, Mobile Money workflows, protected bookings, and owner tools together in one service.

---

## 2. The Problem It Solves
- **For guests:** Accommodation details in Ghana are often scattered across phone calls, social media pages, and word of mouth. It is hard to compare prices, confirm real availability, or get a reliable booking confirmation.
- **For property owners:** Many small and medium accommodation providers still track bookings, room availability, and finances manually. This leads to double-bookings, lost records, and extra work.

For a detailed comparison with existing platforms, see [Competitive Analysis](./research/competitive-analysis.md).

---

## 3. Who Uses FindStay
1. **Guests:** Search and compare verified stays across Ghana, view real availability, book rooms, and pay securely using Mobile Money or bank cards.
2. **Property Owners & Staff:** Verify their identity and property, publish room listings with photos and prices, manage room calendars, and track bookings and payouts.
3. **Platform Administrators:** Review owner verification documents, approve listings before they go live, manage user accounts, and monitor activity logs.

---

## 4. Delivery Plan (Two Phases)
- **Phase 1 (Sprints 1–3):** Owner verification, property listings, room availability calendars, guest search, booking, Mobile Money / card payments, reviews, and payouts.
- **Phase 2 (Sprints 4–5):** Long-term rentals, maintenance tracking, accounting tools, and student hostels.

---

## 5. How the Project Is Organized
- **`web/`** — The FindStay web application used by guests, property owners, and administrators.
- **`api/`** — The backend server that handles business rules, permissions, and the database.
- **`docs/`** — Project requirements, sprint plans, decisions, meeting notes, and test evidence.
- **`showcase/`** — The **FindStay Progress Showcase** website used to present sprint progress.
