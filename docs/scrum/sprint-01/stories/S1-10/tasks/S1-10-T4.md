## 1. Outcome & Objective

Seed clear, plain-language notification templates into PostgreSQL (`notification.notification_template`) for all Sprint 1 lifecycle events (staff invites, verification decisions, listing review/approval outcomes, and account alerts) and implement a robust template placeholder rendering engine within `NotificationSender` / `NotificationService` so FindStay sends consistent, reliable messages without hardcoding copy in business code.

## 4. Template Rendering Engine (`TemplateRenderer`)

- **Component:** `com.findstay.notification.internal.TemplateRenderer`
- **Features:**
  - Fast regex-based placeholder evaluation: `{{\s*([a-zA-Z0-9_.-]+)\s*}}`.
  - **Safe Missing Placeholder Handling:** Automatically replaces unprovided placeholders with empty string `""` without crashing or throwing exceptions.
  - **Special Character Safety:** Uses `Matcher.quoteReplacement(...)` to prevent `$` and `\` from triggering `IllegalArgumentException`.
  - **Seam Integration:** Integrated into `NotificationSender` so `send(...)` automatically resolves in-app templates if title/message are omitted or contain placeholders.
  - **Cross-Channel Method:** Exposes `renderTemplate(eventType, channel, data)` on `NotificationService` for downstream email and SMS dispatchers.
