You run the meeting that starts the sprint properly: walk everyone through the plan and the clarified tasks, confirm the design decisions, hand out the work, and set the Day 2 and Day 3 checkpoints where the team reviews progress together. - Written notes with the Day 2 and Day 3 checkpoint dates — everyone knows what to achieve and by when.
