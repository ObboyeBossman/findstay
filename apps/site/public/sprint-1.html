The goal

One owner. One listing. Live.

By the end of Sprint 1, a verified owner can sign up, build an organisation, submit documents, create a listing with photos and a calendar — and an administrator can approve it on the live system. Tap each step to see what happens.

Who this is for

R1 is for invited property owners and the FindStay admin team. It gives them a safe first way to add and approve places to stay. Guests will not use this release to book yet.

What we are proving

We are proving the full owner journey works: join, show who you are, add a place, add rooms and photos, choose available dates, and get approved.

What can wait

Taking bookings and payments, monthly or school-term stays, calendar links, AI help, and text-to-speech are all later work. Keeping them out helps us finish this first release well.

Product vision

A marketplace that keeps property operations together.

For small and medium accommodation owners and managers, FindStay is a Ghana-focused accommodation marketplace and property-management system that helps them attract verified guests, prevent double-bookings, accept Mobile Money and card payments, and manage occupancy and records in one mobile-friendly platform. Unlike agents, global booking platforms, and standalone PMS software, FindStay brings local verification, Mobile Money workflows, protected bookings, and owner tools together in one service.

The Architecture

Every tool earns its place

Nothing decorative. Each layer has one job and gets out of the way when the work needs to breathe. The screens never touch business tables directly; Supabase Auth owns sign-in; Spring Modulith keeps every part in its own boundary.

Roles & Priorities

Sets the order of work, makes open decisions, accepts or rejects each story at Sprint Review.

Developers

Plan and build the sprint, own quality, release, and demonstrate. Two tracks: A (platform) and B (screens).

Priorities & the unfinished-work rule

  • Every story is Must, Should or Could. Work follows that order each day.
  • A story ships whole or not at all. Unfinished work stays hidden behind its on/off switch.
  • A requirement counts as delivered only when its story is Done at Sprint Review.
  • An unfinished Must story in Sprints 1 to 3 blocks dependent stories; the Product Owner decides at the Day 5 check.
  • Should and Could stories may slide to a later sprint. Moving anything past week 10 needs a formal change request.
  • Reserve 10% of every sprint for fixing the previous release, and 15% in Sprint 4.
